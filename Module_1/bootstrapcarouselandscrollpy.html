Forest

A forest is a large, dense ecosystem filled with trees, plants, and wildlife that provides vital oxygen, clean air, and natural habitats for millions of species.

Ocean

The ocean is a vast body of saline water that covers roughly 70% of the Earth's surface and holds about 97% of the planet's water.

Desert

Deserts are dry regions that receive very little rainfall, usually less than 10 inches (25 cm) per year.
